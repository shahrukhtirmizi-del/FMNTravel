I have been a self-employed business owner since 2014. I changed my career and launched Forget Me Not Travel in April 2024 and it’s the best decision I have made in the last 10 years!

I work within a network of Independent Travel Business owners which allows us to book travel as agents and earn commission on everything that we book.

How it works

Every booking that is made, either directly with a hotel or through a booking site has commission built in. By becoming an Independent Travel Agent you can earn commissions on your own travel, as well as earning commission by booking for friends and family too.

Joining this business will enable you to travel more and provide you with the skills to be smarter when booking your own travel, all whilst being paid to do it.

Whether you want to develop a thriving business whilst building a team of agents, just book your own travel and earn commissions or be self-employed and provide an Independent Travel Agent service this is the business for you.

As soon as you do your basic training, you can start selling travel to anyone, including yourself. Book cruises, vacations, hotels, all-inclusive resorts, package holidays, hotels, staycations, theme park tickets, honeymoons, flights, car rentals, airport parking and so much more.

It works like all other travel search sites, but with access to exclusive offers, competitive rates, insider travel knowledge, destination events, free support, which includes selling tools, weekly webinars, podcasts, live events and so much more.

What you need to know before we chat

  • This is not a get rich quick scheme, it takes time to learn and train, at your own pace. This is a sustainable business opportunity.

  • This is a commission only income and is paid 30-60 days after your client travels.

  • You don’t need to leave your job to start, this can be something that you can start as a side hustle to build up your confidence and your client base.

  • Share the travel business opportunity with others to help them benefit too. There is a multi-level marketing opportunity if you decide to build a team, which you can mentor and develop.

  • This opportunity is not free. There is a fee approx. £40 per month that gives you access to all booking platforms and training – this includes ABTA training and protection.

  • There are no contracts, no sales targets and you can stop at any time.
