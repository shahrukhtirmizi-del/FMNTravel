About Forget Me Not Travel

“I have always loved to travel so I decided to turn my passion for travel into my day job”

My name is Mandy, I am the founder of Forget Me Not Travel and I live in Surrey with my amazing partner Tony, I have 3 grown-up children Luke, Hannah and Daniel and we are also blessed with super amazing grandchildren.

In December 2023, my life changed following the death of my father, I took a few months out to think about my future and made one of the best decisions of my life. After a successful 20+ year career working for the NHS I decided to take the leap and in April 2024 I launched my new travel business,

Forget Me Not Travel.

I have always loved to travel so I decided to turn my passion for travel into my day job, I am now working hard doing what I love to do the most, helping people plan amazing holidays. I already have a loyal customer base and my flexibility to work around your business and your family allows my business to remove some of your added stress whilst travel planning.
